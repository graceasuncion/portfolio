About

How I think, work & what I’m up to

✦Hello! I’m Grace.

A product designer with a background in Cognitive Science and Design from UC Davis. I design human-centered experiences at the intersection of people and AI.

I believe the best design comes from connection. I care about building communities and creating work that brings people together, whether it’s a product that helps someone feel more confident or a space where people feel they belong.

At UC Davis, I spent much of my time in leadership roles, connecting with people across many communities. Those connections shaped how I see people, and how I design for them.

As AI changes how we use technology, I want to make sure it stays human, with experiences that feel personal, thoughtful, and made for the people using them.
